Connection help

Open WebsiteBlocker → Settings → Connect browser. The app prepares its browser connection for you; no commands or extension ID are needed. Then follow its short browser setup steps.

Rules affect HTTP(S) page navigations. Existing pages are affected on reload or navigation. Private mode needs separate browser consent.
